# Knowledge Base Readiness Decision

## Decision

**B. BATCH 5 REQUIRED BEFORE PREPROCESSING**

## Basis for Decision

The current knowledge base contains 44 accepted sources and has strong coverage for many required areas, including:

- Computer Engineering department information
- OSTIM curriculum and course information
- general programming requirements
- broad career opportunities
- employment trends
- skills and technologies
- AI and cybersecurity pathways
- internships in general
- graduate studies
- specializations
- future technologies
- international career preparation
- common misconceptions

However, preprocessing should wait until a small, targeted Batch 5 closes the most important evidence gaps. The issue is not source count. The issue is that several high-value student questions still require more direct evidence.

## Questions That Should Remain Cautious Even After Batch 5

Some questions should never be answered as absolute guarantees:

- "Is Computer Engineering future-proof?"
- "Which specialization is best?"
- "Will AI replace engineers?"
- "Can I work abroad?"
- "Can I get a good job with low GPA?"
- "How long does it take to become senior?"

The future RAG system should answer these with source-grounded nuance, not certainty.

## Readiness Conclusion

The knowledge base is close, but not yet ready for content acquisition and preprocessing. A focused Batch 5 should be completed first to reduce weak evidence in high-stakes advisory areas: Turkey employment, Turkey salaries, GPA/project hiring evidence, no-internship outcomes, workload/stress, AI impact, and Turkey-specific diversity/participation evidence.

After Batch 5, the project can move into content acquisition, raw-file storage, preprocessing design, and chunking strategy.
