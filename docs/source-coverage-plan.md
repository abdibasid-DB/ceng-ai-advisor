# Source Coverage Plan

This plan defines what evidence should be collected later for each knowledge-base category. It is based on the WEX428 guideline and the 60 representative questions in `ceng-questions.pdf`.

No web content has been scraped or downloaded in this stage. No source URLs or source titles are invented here.

## Source Priority Order

When several sources are available, prefer them in this order:

1. Official OSTIM Technical University sources
2. Official university curriculum/course pages
3. Professional engineering/computing organizations
4. Academic papers and conference publications
5. Official technical documentation
6. Government or recognized labor/employment data
7. Major industry/technology reports
8. Reliable educational and career guidance resources

## Coverage Review

All categories have at least two planned sources. High-priority categories have at least three, and broad or fast-changing categories have four.

The areas that most need recent evidence are Employment, Salaries, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Skills and Technologies, Specializations, Future Technologies, and Remote Work.

Some categories overlap naturally. For example, Artificial Intelligence overlaps with Future Technologies and Career Opportunities, while Internships overlaps with Employment and Skills and Technologies. During source collection, each source should receive one primary category and may mention secondary relevance in its notes.
