Curated English note of src_116: OSTİM Technical University academic advising for students

This is a curated English note of src_116, the OSTİM Teknik Üniversitesi "Öğrencilere Sağlanan Akademik Danışmanlık Hizmetleri Yönergesi" (Directive on Academic Advising Services for Students; Senate 09.12.2024 no. 140, Board of Trustees 20.02.2025 no. 84). It is a faithful English translation of the relevant articles, not an official translation; the Turkish terms are in brackets. Advisor assignment (Article 6): every student starting in a program is assigned an academic advisor (akademik danışman) by the head of department, from the department's full-time faculty members and instructors. Assignments are made at the start of each academic year. The advisor can be changed as the student or the department finds appropriate. In principle the assigned advisor follows the student throughout their studies.

Types of advising (Article 5): academic success advising (introduction to university life during the preparatory class and the first semester; academic advising on course selection and success, including double major and minor; ERASMUS advising; Achievers Program advising from the start of the 5th to the end of the 8th semester); entrepreneurship and workplace training advising (workplace practice and adaptation to working life, entrepreneurship, and graduation and career planning, which starts in the 7th semester for graduating students); and project and thesis advising for graduate students.

Course registration (Article 7): each semester, students complete course registration within the announced periods and have it approved by their advisor through the student information system (OBS), both at registration and in the add-drop period. A registration without advisor approval is invalid even if the fees have been paid.

Responsibilities (Article 10): the student must contact their advisor at least once every semester. The advisor advises on course choices and on planning all education processes, informs and guides students on adapting to university life, professional development and careers, helps with problems in university processes, and checks whether the student meets the graduation requirements. KARPİEM (the career planning office) advises students on career goals, informs them about job and internship opportunities, and organises training on job applications and interviews, seminars and career fairs.
