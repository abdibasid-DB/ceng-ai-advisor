Curated English note: OSTİM Technical University applied workplace courses instead of a compulsory internship

This is a curated note of four official OSTİM Technical University sources: src_110 "Kariyer Deneyim Programı Usul ve Esasları" (Career Experience Program Procedures; Senate 27.08.2025 no. 156, Board of Trustees 28.08.2025 no. 89), https://www.ostimteknik.edu.tr/web/content/520586?unique=f784695e6d72fa11e2a77bd327356b881d874b91&download=false; src_109 "Uygulamalı Dersler Yönergesi" (Applied Courses Directive), https://www.ostimteknik.edu.tr/web/content/319109?unique=76e5794fccab6fb1d9111a74d0dc14be366a7368&download=false; and the Computer Engineering curriculum and course contents pages src_003 and src_004, https://mfcs.ostimteknik.edu.tr/course-contents. The Turkish documents are translated faithfully, not officially, with the Turkish terms in brackets; English sentences are quoted exactly.

No compulsory internship (src_110, item 1, translated): there is no "Compulsory Internship" (Zorunlu Staj) process at OSTİM Technical University ("Üniversitemiz bünyesinde 'Zorunlu Staj' süreci bulunmamaktadır."). The Career Experience Program (Kariyer Deneyim Programı) is voluntary and can be used only once: it lasts 20 working days, students find the company themselves, the GPA must be at least 1.80, and faculty students can do it in the summer holidays of the 2nd and 3rd year (items 2, 5, 6, 10 and 13).

Applied courses (src_109): an applied course (Uygulamalı Ders) is a course of the diploma program in which students take part in practice at companies or service areas, under the responsibility of the course instructor, and which is not vocational training at a company or an internship (staj) (Article 3). In a bachelor's program the training consists of applied training in a total of 7 semesters, and in the last semester it is done full-time at the workplace (Article 15). Students must do it at institutions with a signed protocol (Article 11), may not be absent for more than 20% of the training time (Article 11), and are not paid for it (Article 12). Its ECTS credits are part of the related course (Article 12). KARPİEM coordinates the program (Article 8). Students who have worked in a job related to their program may apply for an exemption (Article 18).

Workplace courses in the Computer Engineering curriculum (src_003, src_004): WED 128 Workplace Education (2nd semester): "Students make observations and experience in the workplace one day of the week." WAP 227, WAP 228 and WAP 327 Workplace Application I–III (3rd to 5th semester): "students continue their workplace experience which they started in the previous semester with their mentors." WEX 328 Workplace Experience I (6th semester): "students practice in the workplace one day of the week." WEX 427 Workplace Experience II (7th semester). WEX 428 Workplace Experience III (8th semester): "In the last semester of the program, all of the students will carry out practical training in a factory / production unit located in all provinces of Ankara or in Ankara".
