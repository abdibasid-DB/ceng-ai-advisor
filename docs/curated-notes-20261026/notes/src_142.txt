Curated English note of src_107: OSTİM Technical University English preparatory school, levels, exemption and maximum duration

This is a curated English note of src_107, the "OSTİM Teknik Üniversitesi Yabancı Diller Yüksekokulu Eğitim-Öğretim ve Sınav Yönetmeliği" (School of Foreign Languages Education and Examination Regulation; Resmî Gazete 07.09.2025, no. 33010; in force from the start of the 2025-2026 academic year). It is a faithful English translation of the relevant articles, not an official translation; the Turkish terms are in brackets. Duration (Article 5): the normal length of the English preparatory class (hazırlık sınıfı) is one academic year and the maximum is two academic years. Students who cannot complete the preparatory class successfully within two academic years are dismissed from the program (programdan ilişiği kesilir); on request they are given three exam rights to be used within three years, at the proficiency exams held at the start of the academic year. An academic year has at least four modules; a module is an eight-week period.

Proficiency exam and exemption (Article 6): newly registered students take the English proficiency exam (yeterlik sınavı) at the start of the academic year. The pass mark is at least 60 out of 100. Students go directly to their program if they have the minimum scores set by the Senate in accepted equivalent exams, in central foreign language exams accepted by YÖK, or in YDS; if they completed at least the last three years of secondary education in a country where English is the native language; if they completed secondary education in an IB program; or if they completed the university's preparatory class before and register within three years.

Placement and levels (Articles 7 and 8): a placement exam (düzey belirleme sınavı) is held once a year; students who do not take it start at Pre-A1. Students are placed in five levels: Pre-A1, A1, A2, B1 and B2; after B2 they may continue to C1 and C2. Weekly class hours are at least twenty.

Completing the preparatory class (Articles 9 and 10): a level is passed with a weighted average of at least 60. To take the end-of-year exam a student must complete B2 in the last module with at least 60, or finish the last module at B1 with 70. The end-of-year exam must be passed with at least 60; the overall grade is 40% of the average of the completed levels plus 60% of the end-of-year exam.

Other rules: students of English-medium programs may choose optional preparation even if they pass the proficiency exam (Article 11); a paid, optional summer school of at least six weeks may be opened for unsuccessful students (Article 13); attendance of 85% is required in each module (Article 14).
