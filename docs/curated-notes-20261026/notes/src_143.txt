Curated English note of src_104: OSTİM Technical University scholarships and tuition discounts

This is a curated English note of src_104, the OSTİM Teknik Üniversitesi "Burs ve İndirim Yönergesi" (Scholarship and Discount Directive; Board of Trustees decision 09/03/2023 no. 70, last amended 20/08/2026 no. 94; in force from the 2023-2024 academic year). It is a faithful English translation of the relevant articles, not an official translation; the Turkish terms are in brackets. YKS scholarship quotas (Article 4(1)): full (100%) scholarship quotas (burslu kontenjan) are not less than 15% of a program's total quota in that year's YKS guide; discounted quotas (indirimli kontenjan) are 50% or 25%, as set in the YKS guide.

Academic success discount (Article 4(14)): bachelor's students in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd year with a cumulative GPA of 3.50 or higher out of 4 receive a 10% discount for the following academic year. Preparatory class students and full-scholarship students are excluded. Conditions: not having exceeded the normal duration, no disciplinary penalty, having taken all courses of the program, and no repeated courses and no FF or NA grades.

Other discounts (Article 4): sibling discount 10%; disability discount 10% (disability rate 40% or more); corporate affiliation discount 35% (OSTİM Foundation, OSTİM Technical University, OSTİM Technopark and related bodies, their staff and relatives); national athlete scholarship 100%; sports success discount (national 1st place 50%, 2nd 35%, 3rd 20%; international 1st 75%, 2nd 60%, 3rd 40%); athlete registration scholarship (individual sports 15%, team sports 10%); horizontal transfer discount of 100%, 75%, 50% or 25% depending on the central placement score.

Duration (Article 5): scholarships and discounts are valid for 4 years in bachelor's programs, plus 1 year for programs with compulsory preparatory education; full (100%) scholarships are valid for one more year beyond the normal duration. After that period, a fee per ECTS is charged.

Combining discounts (Article 5): discounts are applied one after another on the tuition fee, and the total discount can never exceed 75% (the yearly fee cannot be less than 25% of the full fee), except for students with a 100% High Achievement Scholarship.

Application (Article 6): except for the YKS/DGS quotas and the academic success discount, discounts require an application by the end of the registration dates or the add-drop week; they are not applied retroactively.

Financial and social aid (Article 8): students in need apply to the Student Affairs Department each academic year with documents showing low family income; they must have no disciplinary penalty and no other scholarship or discount.

Outstanding achievement support (Article 10): students placed in "Burslu" (scholarship) programs within the first 2,500 in the success ranking receive support whose amount, duration and conditions are set by the Board of Trustees each year.
