Curated English note of src_105 and src_106: OSTİM Technical University double major and minor eligibility

This is a curated English note of two OSTİM Teknik Üniversitesi directives: src_105 "Çift Anadal Yönergesi" (Double Major Directive) and src_106 "Yandal Yönergesi" (Minor Directive), both accepted by the Senate on 10.02.2025 (no. 145), put into force by the Board of Trustees on 20.02.2025 (no. 84) and amended on 28.08.2025 (no. 89). It is a faithful English translation of the relevant articles, not an official translation; the Turkish terms are in brackets. Source URLs: https://www.ostimteknik.edu.tr/web/content/520560?unique=c8f94c6d6fd0e7afec145c78ee9022efb0d52a19&download=false (double major) and https://www.ostimteknik.edu.tr/web/content/520575?unique=c0c651e71348ae2e54165dda030486cde985bab0&download=false (minor)

Double major (çift ana dal, ÇAP), eligibility (src_105, Article 8): a student may apply at the earliest at the start of the 3rd semester and at the latest at the start of the 5th semester. The cumulative GPA must be at least 70 out of 100 (at least 2.72 out of 4), and all courses taken so far must have been passed. The student must be in the top 20% of the success ranking of their program; students outside the top 20% may also apply if their score is not below the base score of the double major program in that year. For English-medium programs, the English proficiency conditions must be met. The student must have no disciplinary penalty. Applications are ranked by 60% of the GPA plus 40% of the YKS score. Students placed through DGS cannot do a double major in programs with a success-ranking requirement (Faculty of Engineering).

Double major, program and continuation (src_105, Articles 5 and 14): a bachelor's double major program has at least 240 ECTS, including common or equivalent courses. Only one double major, plus at the same time one different minor, can be followed. The student is dismissed from the double major if the main program GPA falls below 65 out of 100 (2.50 out of 4); graduating from the double major requires a main program GPA of at least 70 out of 100 (2.72 out of 4).

Minor (yan dal), eligibility (src_106, Article 8): a student may apply at the earliest at the start of the 3rd semester and at the latest at the start of the 6th semester. The cumulative GPA must be at least 65 out of 100 (at least 2.50 out of 4), and all credit courses taken so far must have been passed. English proficiency (for English-medium programs) and no disciplinary penalty are required. Applications are ranked by 60% of the GPA plus 40% of the YKS score. Faculty of Engineering students placed through DGS can do a minor in engineering programs.

Minor, program and certificate (src_106, Articles 5 and 14): a minor has at least 6 courses and not less than 30 ECTS, excluding courses common with the main program. Students who complete it receive a minor certificate (yan dal sertifikası), which does not replace a diploma. The student is dismissed from the minor if the main program GPA falls below 60 out of 100 (2.29 out of 4).

Fees (src_105 and src_106, Article 15): after the normal duration of the main program, double major and minor students pay a course fee per ECTS.
