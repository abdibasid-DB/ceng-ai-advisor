# Source Collection Batch 05

## Stage

Source Collection - Batch 5: Final Targeted Evidence Gap Collection

## Project

AI-Powered Computer Engineering Student Advisor

## Date Accessed

2026-08-30

## Scope

This batch was driven by the gaps identified in the coverage and readiness audits. It focused only on Turkey-specific ICT evidence, salary limitations, GPA/projects and hiring evidence, internship gaps, workload/stress, AI impact, women in ICT, mathematics/beginner concerns, and common misconceptions.

No preprocessing, chunking, embeddings, ChromaDB, Ollama, RAG pipeline, chatbot logic, frontend features, or backend features were implemented.

## Rejected Candidates

### rejected_001

- **Candidate:** Random salary websites for "Computer Engineering salary Türkiye" and "software engineer salary Turkey"
- **Reason rejected:** They gave salary numbers without sufficiently transparent methodology, sampling, or official status. The exact Turkey Computer Engineering salary gap remains documented.

### rejected_002

- **Candidate:** Company-specific software engineer salary pages
- **Reason rejected:** Too narrow for general student advising and not reliable for national salary claims.

### rejected_003

- **Candidate:** İŞKUR search-result snippets and inaccessible PDFs
- **Reason rejected:** İŞKUR remains a preferred source, but no accessible source was verified with enough inspectable ICT/software-specific content in this batch.

### rejected_004

- **Candidate:** Bootcamp marketing articles about "projects beat GPA"
- **Reason rejected:** Marketing-oriented and often unsupported. The project should not claim projects always matter more than GPA.

### rejected_005

- **Candidate:** Blogs ranking "best" specializations
- **Reason rejected:** Most made unsupported ranking claims and ignored personal fit, geography, and evidence limitations.

### rejected_006

- **Candidate:** Forum threads about burnout, low GPA, and no internships
- **Reason rejected:** Useful for understanding concerns but not reliable factual authority.

### rejected_007

- **Candidate:** Sensational AI replacement articles
- **Reason rejected:** Rejected because they made extreme claims without balanced labor-market or software-engineering evidence.

### rejected_008

- **Candidate:** Older computing education papers before 2020
- **Reason rejected:** Some were credible, but newer peer-reviewed and university guidance sources were available for this batch.
