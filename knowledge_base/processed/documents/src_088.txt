Curated source note

OSTIM Technical University lists an English thesis-based master's program in Computer Engineering through the Institute of Science. This is an OSTIM-specific graduate-study source and should be used for local MSc questions, while current admissions, fees, and application dates should be checked on official university pages.

The program syllabus page identifies graduate-level Computer Engineering coursework, including advanced technical subjects such as Advanced Computer Networks and other graduate electives. The thesis-based structure indicates a research-oriented path, which is different from only taking undergraduate courses or doing short professional training.

For prospective undergraduate students, this source helps explain that Computer Engineering can continue into graduate study. A master's degree can support deeper specialization, research preparation, academic careers, and advanced technical roles. It is not necessary for every software job, but it may matter more for research-heavy AI, advanced systems, academic work, or specialized engineering roles.

Student-facing guidance: students considering MSc or PhD study should build strong undergraduate foundations, maintain good academic habits, seek projects with faculty supervision when possible, read technical papers gradually, and choose electives/projects that point toward a research area.
