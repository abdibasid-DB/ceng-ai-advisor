1.4Developer roles

Developer type

Full-stack, back-end, and front-end developers were the top three roles reported by developers for the last three years; however, front-end developers have decreased from 6.6% to 5.6% since last year.
This year, more developers identify as students than as front-end employees and we saw a slight increase in developers who have an embedded applications role or are academic researchers.

Which of the following describes your current job, the one you do most of the time? Please select only one.

58,950 (90.1%)
