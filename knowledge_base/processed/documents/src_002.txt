Mission and Vision

Vision

To be a respected department that hosts scientists who are experts in their fields, provides a research and education environment that will create added value for future technologies in computer engineering, and conducts R&D and P&D at national and international standards.

Mission

To train computer engineers who have acquired learning and research skills, who are technically competent enough to use their knowledge effectively on the day they graduate, and who are sensitive to environmental and social problems in an environment supported by advanced technology and intertwined with industry.
