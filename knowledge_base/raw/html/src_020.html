Prime and subcontracting

Contracting counseling and help

Get free guidance from SBA experts and partners to navigate federal contracting and grow your small business.

Procurement Center Representatives

Procurement Center Representatives (PCRs) are SBA professionals who serves as the SBA’s front-line advocate to ensure that all qualified small businesses are afforded the maximum opportunity to compete for federal contracting.

Commercial Market Representatives

Commercial Market Representatives (CMRs) ensure small businesses get their fair share of opportunities as subcontractors. They monitor and support compliance with small-business subcontracting plans, along with teaching agencies and primes about these requirements.

Need help?

  • Get help with prime contracting at contracting@sba.gov
  • Get help with the subcontracting program at subcontracting@sba.gov

Additional resources

  • Small Business Development Centers (SBDCs): One-on-one and low-cost training on contracting, market research, and program support (e.g., 8(a)).
  • Subcontracting Program Assistance (SPA): Tools and guidance to match primes with small subcontractors and help navigate subcontracting relationships.
  • SBA Learning Center: Free online courses and videos to build contracting know-how.
  • APEX Accelerators (formerly PTACs): Help determine readiness, register for procurement systems, pursue opportunities, and find bids.
  • SCORE: Free counseling and workshops from volunteer business mentors.

Trade tools for international sales

Find out how to turn your small business into an export company, grow profits, and access two-thirds of the world’s purchasing power with SBA programs, research, tips and training. SBA Trade Tools helps you learn about our export finance programs, how to develop your export plan, identify your market, and know the laws and regulations of your exporting country. You can find training on international trade from a variety of government and non-governmental sources.
